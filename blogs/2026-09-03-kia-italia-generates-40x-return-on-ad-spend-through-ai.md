KIA Italia has partnered with Infobip to reduce the time between viewing an advertisement and purchasing a car. Thanks to an AI-powered conversational sales journey on WhatsApp, the company generated 144 qualified leads, eight direct vehicle sales and a return on ad spend (ROAS) of 40x.
