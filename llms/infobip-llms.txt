# Infobip

Infobip is a global cloud communications platform that enables businesses to build connected experiences across all stages of the customer journey, with AI as the driving force of innovation.

## Products

- [SMS](https://www.infobip.com/sms): Global SMS messaging for notifications, marketing, and 2FA
- [WhatsApp Business](https://www.infobip.com/whatsapp-business): WhatsApp Business API for support and automated notifications
- [RCS](https://www.infobip.com/rcs): Rich Communication Services with interactive buttons and carousels
- [Viber Business](https://www.infobip.com/viber-business): Viber Business Messages and Viber Bots
- [Voice](https://www.infobip.com/voice): Programmable voice calls, IVR, SIP trunking, number masking
- [Video](https://www.infobip.com/video): Programmable video calling and WebRTC
- [Email](https://www.infobip.com/email): Transactional and marketing email delivery
- [MMS](https://www.infobip.com/mms): Multimedia messaging with images, audio, and video
- [Mobile Push and In-App](https://www.infobip.com/mobile-push): Push notifications and in-app messages for mobile apps
- [Live Chat](https://www.infobip.com/live-chat): Real-time chat on your website or mobile app
- [Apple Messages for Business](https://www.infobip.com/apple-messages-for-business): Communicate with customers on Apple devices
- [Instagram](https://www.infobip.com/instagram-business): Interact and chat with followers via Instagram DM
- [Messenger](https://www.infobip.com/messenger): Facebook Messenger for business communication
- [KakaoTalk](https://www.infobip.com/kakao-business): Communicate with customers through KakaoTalk channels
- [Telegram](https://www.infobip.com/telegram): Set up chatbots and support through Telegram
- [LINE](https://www.infobip.com/line): Connect with customers via LINE messaging
- [Zalo](https://www.infobip.com/zalo): Connect with customers via Zalo in Vietnam
- [Number Lookup](https://www.infobip.com/number-lookup): Validate mobile numbers and improve delivery rates
- [Mobile Identity](https://www.infobip.com/mobile-identity): Silent mobile verification, number intelligence, SIM swap detection
- [Signals](https://www.infobip.com/signals): Block artificial OTP traffic
- [Numbers and Short Codes](https://www.infobip.com/numbers-and-short-codes): Buy and manage phone numbers in 150+ countries
- [CPaaS X](https://www.infobip.com/cpaas-x): Modular API platform with application/entity management
- [Network APIs](https://www.infobip.com/network-apis): Telco network capabilities for authentication and fraud prevention
